Our aim

We see you've found JayaCo. Welcome to a truly environmentally sustainable fashion label. Have you ever considered or stopped to wonder about the impact your shopping habits have on this planet? It is truly sad that as the rate of consumerism increases, so does the waste produced. Moreover, due to steep increases in demand the ethics of it all is often overlooked. Here at JayaCo, we aim to provide a more green and ethical choice when it comes to shopping for designer apparel.

What you can expect

At this point, maybe you are thinking that this entire idea sounds unrealistic and that we are just joking. However, we would like to assure you that at JayaCo, we take things very seriously. From making sure our employees work in fair conditions with perfectly reasonable wages, to making sure you know just how much carbon footprint is associated with the clothing or apparel that we sell. We want you to know exactly what goes into the production of the items you buy such as its environmental impact, the people involved from the start to the finish and their work environment along with ours.
