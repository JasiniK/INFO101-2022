Frequently Asked Questions

How are suppliers chosen?

We run thorough background checks on supply chains to examine their work ethics, employee wage and working conditions. Furthermore, JayaCo choose suppliers that have the same aim as us that is the make ethically produced and environmentally sustainable fashion.

How is the Carbon Footprint calculated?

Based on metrics such as shipping distance from production site to main distribution centre and the amount of waste produced. By applying a factor to both of these metrics according to their environmental impact a carbon footprint score is calculated for each item of clothing. This score is used to calculate your carbon footprint according to the number of clothing you wish to purchase.

Why should I care about my carbon footprint?

During these recent years, there has been an increase in climate change and pollution around the world. Doing our part, even if really small, matters. That is why we created this platform to raise awareness and help give people the choice of sustainability.

Are all the clothes on sale ethnically sourced?

JayaCo makes sure that ourselves, the suppliers and companies that we associate with treat their employees with respect, providing them with good working conditions and at least a living wage. We also ensure that our products are sold using fair-trade principles.

Do you use eco-friendly packaging?

All our products are packed using 100% biodegradable packaging. We want to show that we indeed do practice what we preach.
